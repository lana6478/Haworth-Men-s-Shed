Why Join Us

The Shed is a place to make things with your hands — and to make friends along the way.

Community

Skill Sharing

Our Story

Haworth Men's Shed provides a safe and friendly environment where men can socialise and work on projects at their own pace. We focus on wellbeing, community, and the joy of making — whether that's restoring furniture, fixing small electronics, or simply lending a hand on someone else's project.
