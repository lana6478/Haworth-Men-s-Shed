Looking Ahead

Future Projects & Goals

We're always looking forward. Here's what we have planned for the coming months.

Upcoming

New Lathe Installation

We are expanding our woodworking area with professional-grade lathes for more intricate wood-turning projects.

Community

Local Park Restoration

Our team will be building new benches and planters for Haworth's public spaces this spring.

Learning

Electronics Workshop

Starting next month, we'll be hosting sessions on basic circuit repair and soldering.

Expansion

Greenhouse Project

Plans are in motion to build a sustainable greenhouse to help with our gardening and planting initiatives.

Get Involved

Help us make it happen

These projects run on the time, tools and energy of our members. However you can help, we'd love to hear from you.

Lend a Hand

Join us on an open day and pitch in on whichever project needs an extra pair of hands.

Share a Skill

Got experience in a trade or craft? Offer a short session and pass it on to other members.

Donate Materials or Tools

Spare timber, tools or equipment gathering dust? They could give one of these projects a head start.

Want to be part of what's next?

Come along on an open day or send the organisers a message to find out more.
